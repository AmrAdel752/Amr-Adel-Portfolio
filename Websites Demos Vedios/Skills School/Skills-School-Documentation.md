# Skills School

**A Real, Live Website for Skills Suad Kafafi International School — ASP.NET MVC 5**

Unlike the concept projects elsewhere in this portfolio, this is a genuine client website: a fully built and deployed site for a real international school, currently live at **skillsschool.runasp.net**.

---

## Overview

Skills School (full name: Skills Suad Kafafi International School) needed a professional web presence that could showcase its multiple curricula, academic life, achievements, and leadership to prospective and current parents. This project delivered a complete, production website covering admissions marketing, school life, staff, and contact — built and deployed for the school's real use.

- **Type:** Real client project — live, in-production website
- **Status:** Live and deployed (skillsschool.runasp.net)
- **Visual direction:** Clean, trustworthy blue-and-white palette with real school photography throughout

## Tech Stack

- ASP.NET MVC 5 (.NET Framework 4.7.2)
- C# / Razor
- Bootstrap & JavaScript

## Key Features

### Home
- Hero banner ("Graduate School of Management — Skills School") with real building photography and a clear "About Us" entry point
- Admissions announcement graphics promoting current enrollment

### Accreditation & Partners
- Four-curriculum showcase — **German School, National School, British School, American School** — communicating the range of academic pathways offered
- Real accreditation and partner-institution logos, reinforcing over twenty years of credibility

### School Stats
- "20 Years of Experience" banner with real, meaningful numbers: certified teachers, successful kids, happy parents, and awards won

### Our Events
- Photo-driven event coverage (Aqua Day, National/October celebrations, end-of-year performances) giving prospective families a real feel for school life

### Meet the Board & Popular Teachers
- Individual leadership profiles (e.g. the Vice Chairman of the Board) with photo, title, and a full written bio
- A "Popular Teachers" staff showcase building trust in the people behind the school

### Gallery
- Filterable photo gallery by curriculum (German / British / National / American School), with a lightbox view for full-size images

### Student Life
- **Extra-Curricular Activities** and **Performing Arts** sections detailing after-school clubs, dance, drama, music, and how students build confidence through performance
- Curriculum filter dropdown so parents can see activities relevant to their child's track

### Footer & Contact
- Real contact details: physical address, phone numbers, and email
- Newsletter signup and quick links to key pages (About, Contact, Privacy Policy, Terms, FAQs)
