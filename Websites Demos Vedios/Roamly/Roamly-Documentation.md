# Roamly

**A Travel & Stay Explorer — Concept Demo**

A warm, editorial travel-planning site that pairs an inspiring destination catalogue with a lightweight trip-planning form. The goal is to make discovering a destination feel like the first step of the trip itself, not a database search.

---

## Overview

Roamly is a static concept site for a travel-discovery platform: browse curated destinations by mood or category, get inspired by real-feeling stories and reader testimonials, and send a simple trip inquiry — all without a live booking backend. Roamly evolved from an earlier static project ("Level") into a fuller destination-and-planning experience.

- **Type:** Front-end concept / portfolio project
- **Status:** Demo — the trip planner, inquiry form, and newsletter are front-end only ("Roamly does not process real reservations, payments, availability, hotel inventory, or traveler accounts")
- **Visual direction:** Warm cream background, deep forest-green primary text/UI, terracotta-orange accent for calls to action

## Tech Stack

- HTML5
- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript

## Key Features

### Home
- Hero ("Find your next unforgettable escape") with quick links to featured destinations and a "Watch Our Story" video prompt
- Rotating category spotlights (e.g. city breaks, weekend escapes) as alternate hero states

### Trip Planner
- Inline search widget: destination, check-in/check-out dates (with a full calendar date-picker), and traveler count (adults/children steppers)
- Framed clearly as a **portfolio concept search** — results point to the featured escapes already on the page rather than a live inventory

### Travel Inspiration
- Four-pillar value section: Curated escapes, Inspiring stays, Local experiences, Easy trip planning
- Supporting stats strip: featured destinations, travel styles, experience categories, and planner simplicity

### Experiences
- Destination-led browsing (e.g. Queenstown, New York) framed around the *type* of experience (adventure, city breaks) rather than just geography

### Destinations
- Filterable destination grid — **All / Beach / City / Nature / Culture**
- Cards show country, a short description, and an "Explore" link through to more detail

### Testimonials
- "Stories from the road": short first-person quotes from named travelers tied to a specific trip style (e.g. "Queenstown mountain escape")

### Our Story
- Brand narrative section ("Travel changes the way we see the world") with a "Play Story" video entry point

### FAQ
- "Before you pack your bags" — direct, transparent answers about the demo's limits (no real bookings, how the planner works, saved destinations, mobile support)

### Contact
- "Let's plan somewhere unforgettable" inquiry form (name, email, subject, message)
- Embedded map panel for location context

### Newsletter
- "Travel ideas, straight to your inbox" email capture

### Footer
- Site navigation grouped by Explore / Company / Project, plus a "Back to top" shortcut and build credit
