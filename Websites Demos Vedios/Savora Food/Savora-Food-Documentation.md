# Savora

**A Modern Food & Recipe Discovery Website — Concept Demo**

A front-end recipe and food-discovery site built around bold black-and-yellow editorial styling. The focus is on making recipes easy to browse, filter, and dig into — with just enough polish (favorites, quick filtering, a newsletter capture) to feel like a real product.

---

## Overview

Savora is a static demo for a recipe/food-blog style website. It combines an editorial homepage (rotating category spotlights) with a fully filterable recipe catalogue and detailed recipe pages that include ingredients, steps, and nutrition-style info.

- **Type:** Front-end concept / portfolio project
- **Status:** Demo — inventory of recipes and interactions (favorites, newsletter) are front-end only
- **Visual direction:** High-contrast black background with bold yellow accent, editorial typography

## Tech Stack

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript

## Key Features

### Home
- Rotating hero banners spotlighting different categories (e.g. "Pizza turns simple moments into rituals", "Desserts make the last bite matter")
- Quick category tabs: Burgers, Pizza, Pasta, Healthy, Desserts

### Explore Categories
- Visual category grid (Burgers, Pizza, Pasta, Healthy, Desserts, Drinks) as an entry point into the recipe catalogue

### Recipe Listings
- Filterable by category (All / Burger / Pizza / Pasta / Healthy / Dessert) and difficulty
- Recipe cards show time, difficulty, rating, and a **View Recipe** action
- Favorite/save toggle on each card

### Popular This Week / Recipe Highlights
- Curated "trending" recipe rail, separate from the main filterable grid, for editorial-style spotlighting

### Recipe Detail Pages
- Full recipe view: hero photo, prep time, difficulty, rating
- Ingredients list
- Step-by-step preparation instructions

### Featured Recipes
- Dedicated search-and-filter section for finding a specific recipe by name or category

### Newsletter & Footer
- "Get Fresh Recipes Weekly" email capture section
- Full site footer with navigation, discovery links (Popular/Latest/Favorites), and social links

## Pages / Sections
