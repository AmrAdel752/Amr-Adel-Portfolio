# Mealora

**A Recipe Discovery Site with an Ingredient Ordering Cart — Concept Demo**

A recipe-discovery site that goes a step further than most: instead of just showing a recipe and its ingredient list, Mealora lets the user add those ingredients straight to an order — turning "what should I cook?" into "what do I need to buy?" in one flow.

---

## Overview

Mealora helps users go from a craving to a finished meal: search or browse recipes by ingredient, view full ingredient breakdowns, save favorites, and — the standout feature — add a recipe's ingredients directly to a priced order/cart, complete with delivery details and a running total.

- **Type:** Front-end concept / portfolio project
- **Status:** Demo — ordering and account features are front-end only; recipe data is sourced from a public recipe API
- **Visual direction:** Warm cream background with a bold orange/red accent

## Tech Stack

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript

## Key Features

### Home
- Hero ("Recipes for every craving") with quick entry points to explore or find a specific recipe
- **Recently Viewed** section for quick return access
- "Start with a familiar craving" category shortcuts (Pizza, Chicken, Cake, Pasta, Chocolate, Breakfast)
- "A few delicious ideas to get you started" — featured recipe picks

### Recipe Search & Browsing
- Free-text search plus a **"Browse by ingredient"** sidebar (Mushrooms, Pumpkin, Apple, Banana, Blueberry, and more) for ingredient-first discovery
- Results grid shows recipe photo, title, and source attribution (e.g. "The Pioneer Woman", "Two Peas and Their Pod")

### Recipe Detail
- Full "What you will need" ingredient list with quantities
- **Order Summary panel** — price, quantity stepper, delivery charge, delivery address, and running total
- **Add to Cart** / **Place Order** actions, with a "Login to Add to Cart" gate for guests
- Save to Favorites from the detail page

### Favorites
- Saved-recipe grid for quick access to recipes the user wants to come back to

### Cart
- "Your Cart" page listing saved/ordered recipes with per-item removal — effectively a grocery cart built from recipe ingredients rather than individual products

### Trust & Process
- "A clearer way to move from idea to meal" — a simple three-step framing: Discover, Cook, Save
- "Find your next favorite recipe" call-to-action banner

## Pages / Sections

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Home | Hero, recently viewed, featured picks |
| Recipes | Search & browse-by-ingredient |
| Recipe Detail | Ingredients + order summary |

## Notes for Portfolio Presentation

- Recipe content and images are sourced from a public recipe API for demo purposes; the ordering, cart, and account features are front-end only.
- The ingredient-to-order flow (turning a recipe's ingredient list into a priced, deliverable cart) is the project's standout feature — most recipe-site clones stop at "view recipe."
