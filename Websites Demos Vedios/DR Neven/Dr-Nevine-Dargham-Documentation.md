# Dr. Nevine Dargham — Dermatology & Cosmetic Clinic

**A Bilingual (Arabic RTL) Medical Aesthetics Website — Concept Demo**

A warm, trust-focused website for a dermatology and laser-cosmetics consultant, built right-to-left in Arabic. The design leans on the doctor's credentials and real-looking before/after results to build confidence, then funnels every path toward one action: booking a consultation.

---

## Overview

This is a static demo site for **Dr. Nevine Dargham**, presented as an Assistant Professor of Dermatology and Consultant of Laser & Cosmetology (Kasr Al Ainy, Cairo University). The site is built fully in **Arabic with right-to-left (RTL) layout**, using a soft cream-and-rose colour palette suited to a medical aesthetics brand.

- **Type:** Front-end concept / portfolio project
- **Status:** Demo — booking, WhatsApp contact, and consultation requests are illustrative only
- **Language/Direction:** Arabic (RTL)
- **Visual direction:** Cream/beige background, rose-pink primary accent, gold secondary accent, warm portrait photography

## Tech Stack

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript

## Key Features

### Home
- Hero section ("اكتشفي جمالك الحقيقي" — *Discover Your True Beauty*) pairing a warm doctor portrait with a clear value statement
- Dual call-to-action: **book a consultation** or **contact via WhatsApp**
- Trust strip: patient count (5000+), "guaranteed results," and years of academic experience

### Services
- Grid of medical & cosmetic treatment categories ("خدمات طبية وتجميلية متميزة" — *Distinguished Medical & Cosmetic Services*), each with an icon and short description

### Before & After Gallery
- Real-style transformation gallery ("تحولات حقيقية تتحدث عن نفسها" — *Real transformations that speak for themselves*)
- Split before/after imagery across treatment areas: forehead lines, lip filler, hands, and pigmentation/skin spots
- Each result is labeled with the treatment applied

### About the Doctor
- Dedicated bio section: name, academic title, university affiliation, and a short professional summary
- Credential cards (academic institution, teaching hospital, specialisation areas)

### Why Choose This Clinic
- Reasons grid covering: modern equipment, natural-looking results, advanced experience, personalised care, strong reputation, and precise follow-up

### Patient Testimonials
- Star-rated patient quotes ("ماذا تقول مريضاتنا؟" — *What do our patients say?*) reinforcing trust before the final call to action

### Call to Action
- Repeated, low-friction booking prompt ("جاهزة لبشرة أكثر إشراقاً؟" — *Ready for more radiant skin?*) with both a direct booking button and a WhatsApp shortcut

### Footer
- Contact details (phone, address, working hours), quick navigation links, and social media icons, on a dark contrasting background to close the page
